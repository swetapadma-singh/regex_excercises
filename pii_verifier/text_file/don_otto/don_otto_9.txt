FLORIDA HIGHWAY PATROL555-000-0002
Apr 29, 2025

Vehicle one (V01) and Vehicle two (V02) were traveling north on State Road 91 north of Mile Post 257. V01 was traveling in the inside lane. V02 was traveling in the outside
lane. The driver of V01 made a lane change from the inside lane to the outside lane into the path of V02. As a result, the front of V02 collided with the right rear area of the
trailer attached to V01. V01 then continued onto the right shoulder where the front of V01 collided with traffic barrels. After collision, V01 came to final rest facing north on
the right shoulder. V02 came to final rest facing west in lane 3.
